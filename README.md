# 💫 About Me:
As a BTech student specializing in Electronics and Communication Engineering at Netaji Subhash Engineering College, I am passionate about building the digital future through VLSI, embedded systems, and IoT innovation.<br><br>My hands-on experience includes:<br>- Designing digital systems using Verilog on Xilinx Vivado<br>- Prototyping IoT solutions with Arduino and ESP32<br>- Simulating analog circuits using NI Multisim<br><br>Certified in VLSI System on Chip Design (Maven Silicon) and VLSI for Beginners (NIELIT Calicut), I am committed to deepening my expertise and contributing to India’s growing semiconductor ecosystem.<br><br>Outside the lab, I am a dedicated tabla player, blending technical precision with artistic creativity—a balance that shapes my approach to engineering and teamwork.<br><br>Currently preparing for GATE 2027, I am eager to connect with peers, mentors, and organizations to collaborate on projects and explore opportunities in semiconductor technology and digital electronics.<br>
